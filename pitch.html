01 / The Fatal Flaw

Semantic Caches Rot Silently.

💰

Pricing Drift

Twitter X, Stripe, or cloud providers update prices. Gateway serves old cached prices with 0.98 similarity.

📦

API Deprecations

SDK method deprecated in v2. Gateway serves v1 snippets, breaking customer production builds.

👔

Leadership & Rules

New CEO appointed or regulatory compliance rules change. Static cache serves obsolete facts.

Operators are forced to either disable semantic caching entirely, or risk serving poisoned truths.
